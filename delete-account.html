Delete your TierLift account and data

TierLift · Android app com.agupitu.TierLift · Last updated 19 September 2026

This page explains how to delete your TierLift account and the data attached to it, what gets deleted, and what is kept.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the TierLift app and go to Profile.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  3. Read what is deleted and what stays, then type your username to confirm.

Deletion is immediate. Your session ends and you are returned to the home screen.

You can also write from the email address you use to sign in to platephoenix.design@gmail.com, put Delete my TierLift account in the subject line, and include the username shown on your profile. Email requests are completed within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and the sign-in link to your Google account.
  • Your email address.
  • Your profile: username, display name, avatar image, follower and following counts.
  • Your tier votes on exercises and routines.
  • Your saved exercises and saved routines.
  • The routines you created and their contents.
  • Who you follow and who follows you.
  • The content reports you submitted.
  • The photos and videos you uploaded.

What is kept, and for how long

DataKeptWhy
Exercises you published Indefinitely, under the public deleted_user profile Other users' routines, saves and tier votes point at them. They stay in the catalog so community rankings and other people's routines keep working.
Aggregated tier scores Indefinitely, with no link to you Ranking totals hold no personal data once your individual vote is removed.
Server and database backups Up to 30 further days after deletion Backups roll over on a fixed schedule and are then overwritten.

If you also want those exercises hidden from the catalog, say so in an email to the address below and they are taken down within 30 days.

Deleting some data without deleting your account

You do not have to close your account to have data removed. Routines you created can be deleted inside the app at any time, from the routine's own screen, and that deletion is immediate.

For anything else — a photo or video you uploaded, an exercise you published, your avatar, your votes, your saves, or your follows — write to platephoenix.design@gmail.com from the email address you sign in with, put Delete my TierLift data in the subject line, and name what you want removed. It is deleted within 30 days, your account stays open, and the same retention rules described in the table above apply: aggregated tier scores survive without any link to you, and backups are overwritten within 30 further days.
