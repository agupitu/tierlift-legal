TierLift — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026 · Applies to the TierLift Android app (com.agupitu.TierLift)

TierLift is a community app where people publish weight-training exercises, rank them into tiers, build routines and follow each other. This policy explains what the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and how to get it deleted.

The short version. TierLift asks Google who you are so you can sign in, then stores the content you choose to publish. It runs no advertising, no analytics and no tracking SDKs. Anything you publish — your profile, your exercises, your photos and videos — is public to every other user.

1. Who is responsible

TierLift is an independent project published by its developer as an individual. Questions about this policy, or requests about your data, go to:

platephoenix.design@gmail.com

2. What the app collects

2.1 Account data

Signing in is done exclusively through Google Sign-In. TierLift does not ask for, see or store a password. When you sign in, Google passes on your email address and your Google account identifier, and TierLift stores them to identify your account across sessions.

2.2 Profile data

On first sign-in the app creates a public profile containing a username derived from your email address, a display name, an optional avatar image, a reputation score, and follower and following counts. Your profile is visible to every other user of the app. Your email address is not shown on your profile and is not visible to other users.

2.3 Content you publish

Everything you create in the app is stored and shown publicly: exercises and their titles, descriptions and steps, muscle and equipment tags, photos and videos you upload, routines you build, and links to third-party videos you reference.

2.4 Activity data

The app records what you do so the rankings and your library work: the tier you vote for on each exercise and routine, the exercises and routines you save, who you follow, the reputation events and achievements you earn, and any content reports you submit to moderation.

2.5 Data stored on your device

The app keeps your session token and a cached copy of recently viewed content in the app's own local storage, so it opens quickly and survives a restart. This data never leaves your device except as part of the normal requests described above, and it is erased when you sign out or uninstall the app.

2.6 What the app does not collect

  • No advertising identifiers, and no advertising of any kind.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or attribution SDKs.
  • No location data.
  • No contacts, calendar, call logs or SMS.
  • No microphone or audio recording.
  • No health, fitness-sensor or biometric data. The app does not track workouts you perform.

3. Camera and photo access

The app asks for access to your camera and your photo library only at the moment you choose to attach an image or a video to an exercise you are creating. Media is uploaded only when you confirm it. If you decline the permission, the rest of the app continues to work and you can still publish exercises using a third-party video link instead.

4. How the data is used

DataPurpose

Tier votesCompute the community ranking of each exercise

None of this data is sold, rented, or used to build advertising profiles.

5. Publicly readable media

Photos and videos uploaded to TierLift are stored in public storage buckets. This means the file can be opened by anyone who has its direct URL, whether or not they use the app or are signed in. Do not upload anything you would not want to be publicly accessible. Removing an exercise removes it from the app, but a file whose URL was copied and shared elsewhere may remain reachable to whoever holds that URL.

6. Who the data is shared with

  • Supabase hosts the database, the file storage and the authentication service behind the app. All account, profile, content and activity data described above is stored there. See the Supabase privacy policy.
  • Google provides the sign-in. Google receives the fact that you authenticated with TierLift. See the Google privacy policy.
  • YouTube serves any third-party exercise video that a user linked. Those videos are embedded through youtube-nocookie.com, which does not set tracking cookies until you press play. See the Google privacy policy.

Data may also be disclosed where a law, a court order or a valid legal request requires it.

7. Retention

Account, profile, content and activity data is kept for as long as your account exists. When an account is deleted, its profile, votes, saves, follows, routines and uploaded media are deleted with it. Exercises you published may be retained without your name attached where other users' routines and votes depend on them, so that the community rankings stay intact.

8. Deleting your account and your data

The app does not yet include a self-service delete button. Until it does, send a deletion request from the email address linked to your account to:

platephoenix.design@gmail.com

Write "Delete my TierLift account" in the subject line. The account and the data listed in section 7 are deleted within 30 days of the request. You may also request a copy of the data held about you, or a correction of it, at the same address.

9. Security

Traffic between the app and the server travels over HTTPS. Database access is restricted by row-level security rules, so a signed-in user can only modify their own records. No system is perfectly secure, and this policy makes no guarantee that a breach can never happen.

10. Children

TierLift is not directed at children or teenagers. The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over, and its Play Store target audience is set accordingly. If you believe a minor has created an account, write to the address above and it will be removed.

11. International transfers

The servers that host TierLift data are operated by Supabase and may be located outside your country of residence. By using the app you accept that your data is processed in those locations.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, restrict or erase your personal data, and to object to its processing. All of these requests go to the contact address above and are answered within 30 days.

13. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published at this address and the date at the top is updated. Substantial changes are announced inside the app.
